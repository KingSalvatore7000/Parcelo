Questions

What committees and residents ask before they start.

Email the Parcelo team

Does Parcelo replace MyGate or NoBrokerHood?

No. Those apps handle visitors and gate approvals. Parcelo handles only parcels: logging, slots, codes and the handover record.

What if a resident loses their Parcelo code?

They reschedule in Parcelo and get a new code. The old one stops working at once.

What happens after 5 wrong codes?

The code locks, so a guessed code never opens a parcel. The resident reschedules to get a fresh one.

How do residents sign in to Parcelo?

With their mobile number and a password. The committee adds them, by hand or from a spreadsheet, and hands over a temporary password they change at first sign-in.

What does door delivery cost?

₹20 a parcel by default. Counter pickup is free.

Can the gate send a parcel back?

Yes. The guard marks it returned to the courier, and any open code stops working.
